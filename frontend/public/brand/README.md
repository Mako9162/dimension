# Logo Taller Dimensión

Prompt utilizado:

> Use case: logo-brand. Create one finished professional logo for an automotive bodywork and paint workshop named exactly "Taller Dimensión" (accent on ó). Clean premium vector-like flat logo on a pure white background, horizontal compact composition, generous but not excessive margin. An original geometric D emblem integrating a subtle car roof contour, followed by a beautifully typeset wordmark: small uppercase TALLER over bold uppercase DIMENSIÓN. Deep forest teal and graphite, matching an elegant workshop management application. Crisp simple shapes, strong legibility at small size, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D mockup, no stationery or scenery, no additional words, no extra variants. The single logo should occupy most of the canvas width. Suitable for a quotation PDF letterhead and web login.
